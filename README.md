BookMyShow Pulse: A Digital Business Systems Analysis and Proposal for an AI-Powered Event Intelligence Platform

Executive Summary
The digital revolution has changed the way businesses conduct themselves and interact with consumers. BookMyShow, one of India's largest entertainment ticketing platforms, has managed to make movie and live event booking much easier and efficient, but been plagued with issues in last minute sales of tickets such as long virtual queues, payment failures, and lack of clarity on bookings. This report investigates the current digital business systems of BookMyShow in terms of TPS, MIS, DSS, Enterprise Systems, and Business Intelligence, and suggests to them an enhancement to their current system using Artificial Intelligence: BookMyShow Pulse, which includes queue intelligence, predictive wait times, dynamic waitlists, management dashboards and tailored recommendations for better customer experience and decision making without replacing their current system.

1. Organization Overview

1.1 Company Overview
BookMyShow is the country's biggest online entertainment ticketing site. The company was established by Ashish Hemrajani in 1999 with the mission of online movie ticket bookings, but has since diversified into live entertainment. Now, BookMyShow offers ticket sales for movies, concerts, sporting events, stand-up comedy shows, theatre performances, festivals and digital entertainment.
It has come a long way since its beginnings as a booking app to become the whole digital entertainment system for the customer, theatres, event organizers, payment providers, advertisers and business partners with its integrated information system.
It attributes its market advantage to the wide range of entertainment partners it has, its reach across the country, its mobile app and its ability to handle millions of transactions in periods of high usage.

1.2 Nature of Business

7.  Proposed Digital Business System Enhancement: BookMyShow Pulse.
It is an Event Intelligence Platform that leverages AI to improve the existing digital ecosystem of BookMyShow, which is entitled ‘BookMyShow Pulse'.
Pulse is not a new booking platform; rather it is an intelligence layer atop of databases and information systems.
The key goal of its is to deliver better decision-making, customer experience and information transparency without impacting on the booking system's core. The main purpose of its is to enhance the decision-making, customer experience and information transparency, while keeping the core of the booking system unchanged.

Core Modules
1. Queue Intelligence Engine
Provides customers with:
   •	Live queue position 
   •	Estimated waiting time 
   •	Ticket availability updates 
This helps minimize uncertainty and increase customer confidence in the sales process when it's busy.

2. Demand Intelligence Engine
Analyses:

8. The BookMyShow Pulse - a system design overview.
BookMyShow Pulse is not meant to replace the existing digital infrastructure of BookMyShow, but rather it's an intelligent supplement. The platform leverages data from booking, customer, payment and events to provide predictive insights for customers, organizers and management.
The suggested system presents a number of interconnected intelligence modules which enhance transparency, personalisation and managerial decision making.

8.1 Notification Intelligence
One of the drawbacks of the existing platform is that users typically find out about events through searching or in vague notifications. BookMyShow Pulse' brings in smart notifications, which can analyse user preferences and suggest user relevant events. For instance, customers who are going to book stand-up comedy shows or a particular category of movies will get alerts before the occurance of more such shows and movies are rolled out to customers. This personalised approach will enhance event discovery, boost customer engagement and shift the way people think about how to discover entertainment from a reactive ticketing platform to a proactive platform.

8.2 Smart Waitlist System
In times of high demand tickets can sell out in minutes. But many times different reasons like canceling, failure to pay, booking timeouts result in empty seats later.
The Smart Waitlist System copies and monitors these spaces automatically and assigns the next in line/first come first served spaces to customers who are waiting on the waitlist according to their position in the queue and waitlist priority.
Customers get instant alerts when seats are available, rather than having to constantly go through the application.
This feature not only provides enhanced levels of customer satisfaction, but also the best possible seat utilization.

8.3 Organizer Intelligence Dashboard
Currently, BookMyShow's booking statistics and sales reports are available to the organizers. Predictive dashboards are an addition to this with Pulse that enable improved event planning.
The dashboard includes:
•	Expected attendance 
•	City-wise demand trends 
•	Booking velocity 
•	Occupancy forecasts 
•	Customer demographics 
•	Well-known booking time periods 
•	Event performance comparisons 
With these insights, organisers can develop a more accurate estimate of the event's audience needs and better plan future events.

9. Proposed Database Design
BookMyShow already has databases for bookings and payments running. BookMyShow Pulse extends this architecture by adding more relational tables, adding support for Predictive Analytics & Business Intelligence.
Proposed Database Tables
•	Users 
•	Events 
•	Venues 
•	Organizers 
•	Bookings 
•	Payments 
•	Queue 
•	Waitlist 
•	Preferences 
•	Recommendations 
•	Notifications 
•	Reviews 
•	Customer Support 
•	Analytics 
Primary and foreign keys are used to connect each table together to ensure there is no duplication of data and has referential integrity.

For example:
There is one User and one Event for each Booking. 
All Recommendations are based on a customer's preference profile. 
Queue records are associated with a queue of booking sessions. 
Relevant information for the notifications is linked with the customer engagement data. 
The database is designed to be normalized, which means that there are no duplicate data or data structures anywhere.

Business Intelligence and Data Warehouse.
Data is collected from the operations, through bookings, and transferred regularly to a centralized analytical repository.
This data warehouse contains historical booking trends, customer behaviour, the popularity of events and regional demand.
This information is analysed using Business Intelligence tools to create dashboards, understand customer preferences and aid managerial decision making.
The analytical database is used for forecasting and long-term planning, unlike the transactional database which is for day-to-day business.

10. Security, Privacy and Ethical Considerations
Confidentiality, integrity and availability of user data is critical for BookMyShow, a digital platform that processes millions of shows per user.
The following security features are important:
•	Secure authentication mechanisms 
•	Password encryption 
•Ensure the integration of payment gateways is secure. 
•Role Based Access Control (RBAC) 
•Backup your data and protect it from disaster. 
•	Continuous system monitoring 
•	Fraud detection mechanisms 
Access to the customer's data must also be in accordance with relevant data protection laws such as the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 in India and international data protection principles.
The recommendation engine should assess customers' behavior with responsibility. Users need to retain control over notification preferences and recommendations cannot be intrusive.
Likewise, the Queue Intelligence Engine is supposed to give realistic waiting time estimates, based on the information that is available at the time, and not fool the users by giving them false hopes.
Ethical use of Artificial Intelligence is crucial to maintaining trust and fairness with customers.

12. Conclusion
By efficiently processing bookings, forming strategic partnerships with industry stakeholders, and managing a scalable digital platform, BookMyShow has carved a niche for itself as the top digital entertainment platform in India. But, recent event with a high demand has identified the need to improve transparency of information, predictive analytics and intelligent decision support with a view to improving the customer experience.
The proposed BookMyShow Pulse system offers solutions to these opportunities by adding an Artificial Intelligence-powered intelligence layer to complement BookMyShow's digital infrastructure. Instead of replacing current systems, Pulse augments them with Queue Intelligence, Demand Prediction, Smart Recommendations, Dynamic Waitlists and Executive Analytics.
The proposed solution is very similar to the concepts of Digital Business Systems, including Transaction Processing Systems, Management Information Systems, Decision Support Systems, Enterprise Systems, Business Intelligence, Digital Transformation. BookMyShow Pulse helps engage customers by increasing information quality and in turn, improve managerial decisions and operational efficiency with proactiveness.
In the era of digital businesses, those that make operational information into intel will be in a stronger position to provide top-tier customer experiences, and to maintain their competitive edge over the long term. As such, BookMyShow Pulse exemplifies the potential for smart information systems to build a more transparent, customized, and efficient entertainment ecosystem.
